You must be wondering why I'd create a site like this, so bare bones, in 2026 no less.

My goal here is simple, practice English (that's why it's English only for now, sorry, maybe that changes in the future) and keep a record of my studies and projects.

Along this journey in tech, I've started many projects, researched many subjects, and over time, through changing machines and changing companies, nothing I built and learned was ever recorded (damn).

So from now on, from the simplest topics to the most complex, from projects that go to production to projects that are just a POC to validate a concept, I'll write it all down here, like a personal diary of everything I've been learning.

Imagine if I'd been doing this since the first time I set up my first Tibia server in 2007 with a C++ engine (The Forgotten Server), a PHP site, and an SQLite database, running on an incredible machine with 512MB of RAM and the Speedy internet of the time hahaha. It would've been funny.

Or all those years working with SIP and Asterisk. Oh lord, how many times did I have to go to a client's place because they couldn't make a single call, something had happened, and we'd lost SSH access? Old school shit (back before AI was a thing).

And especially nowadays, how many projects do we start, how many problems do we solve, how many subjects do we study day to day, only to let it all fall into total oblivion?

No mooore.

See you in the next chapters.
